# EASA Open Category — UAS operations (condensed for RAG)

Summary of the EASA drone regulatory framework (Regulation (EU) 2019/947). Training corpus, not legal
advice. Consult EASA and your member state authority.

## Open category overview
Low-risk civil UAS operations. No prior authorization required if the operation stays within the
open category conditions. Key limits: aircraft MTOM < 25 kg, VLOS always maintained, max altitude
120 m above ground, no carriage of dangerous goods, no dropping of materials endangering persons/
property.

## Subcategory A1 (fly over people)
Aircraft: privately built, or C0 (< 250 g), or C1 (up to 900 g, with C-marking). Operations over
uninvolved people only for C0/privately built; C1 must not intentionally fly over uninvolved people
and must be equipped with low-speed mode. No requirement to stay 150 m from people.

## Subcategory A2 (fly close to people)
Aircraft: C2 class with remote pilot certificate of competency (A2 CofC). Minimum horizontal distance
of 30 m from uninvolved people, reducible to 5 m in low-speed mode. Must not overfly uninvolved people.

## Subcategory A3 (fly far from people)
Aircraft: C2, C3 or C4, or privately built. Only far from people and buildings — keep 150 m from
residential, commercial, industrial or recreational areas.

## Class markings C0–C4
C0 < 250 g, C1 < 900 g, C2 < 4 kg, C3 < 25 kg (large, low-noise), C4 < 25 kg (legacy/unmanned
gliders). C-marked drones sold in EU carry mandatory identification and remote identification (direct
remote ID) from C1 upward.

## Operator registration and remote pilot competency
Operators must be registered with the member state authority (for aircraft ≥ 250 g, or any aircraft
with a camera even below 250 g). Remote pilots need online training + exam for A1/A3; A2 additionally
requires the A2 CofC.

## Geographical zones (U-space and national zones)
Member states designate geographic UAS zones. Some allow operations without authorization, others
require authorization or are prohibited. Check national UAS geographical zone portals (e.g., in Germany
DIPUL, in France Géoportail) before flight.

## Specific category
Operations not possible in the open category require an authorization under the specific category —
SORA (Specific Operations Risk Assessment) or standard scenarios (STS) under Regulation (EU) 2019/947
Art. 11.
